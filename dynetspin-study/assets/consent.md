# Consent

**Study.** Reading change in evolving networks.

**What we collect.** Your answers, how long each task takes, and which parts of
the visualization you interact with (which time period you view, what you hover
over and click). We also collect the background answers on the next page.

**What we do not collect.** Your name, email, IP address, or any other
identifying information. Your participant ID links your answers to your payment
and nothing else.

**Use of the data.** Anonymous, aggregated results will appear in an academic
publication about visualization design. Anonymised raw data may be published
alongside it so other researchers can check our analysis.

**Risks.** None beyond ordinary screen use.

**Voluntary.** You may stop at any time by closing the tab, without giving a
reason. Data from incomplete sessions is discarded.

**Questions.** Contact the address shown at the bottom of this page.

By entering your participant ID and selecting *I consent*, you confirm that you
are 18 or older, that you have read the above, and that you agree to take part.
