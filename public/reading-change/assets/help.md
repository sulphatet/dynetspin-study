# Help

**The picture.** Each dot is one person. Dots are grouped into spirals — one
spiral per group. The size of a spiral shows how many people are in that group.

**Time.** The buttons down the left-hand side are time periods. Click one to see
that period. The highlighted button is the period you are looking at now.

**Moving around.** Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. Hover a dot to see who it is.

**Colour.** What the colour of a dot means changes between tasks, and the legend
on screen always names whichever one you are looking at. There are three:

- **four colours** — where that person sits in time around the period you are
  looking at. **Blue** = just arrived (*Incoming*). **Orange** = about to leave
  (*Outgoing*). **Purple** = passing through, in and out within this period
  (*Transient*). **Grey** = staying (*Stable*). The legend lists only the ones
  the current period can contain;
- **a red-to-blue ramp** (*Volatility*) — how much turnover, across the whole
  span: red is the most, blue the least, yellow in between. Both ends are dark,
  so read the hue and not the darkness;
- **the same ramp labelled *Degree*** — how many connections that person has in
  this period, red most to blue least.

None of the three is the "right" one, and no question depends on knowing which
you have.

**If a task mentions an outlined group**, that group is drawn with a dark ring
around each of its members in the period you start on.

**Stuck?** Give your best answer and continue — a wrong answer is far more
useful to us than a long pause. There is no penalty.
