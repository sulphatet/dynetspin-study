# Help

**The picture.** Each dot is one person. Dots are grouped into spirals — one
spiral per group. The size of a spiral shows how many people are in that group.

**Time.** The buttons down the left-hand side are time periods. Click one to see
that period. The highlighted button is the period you are looking at now.

**Moving around.** Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. Hover a dot to see who it is.

**If a task mentions an outlined group**, that group is drawn with a dark ring
around each of its members in the period you start on.

**Stuck?** Give your best answer and continue — a wrong answer is far more
useful to us than a long pause. There is no penalty.
