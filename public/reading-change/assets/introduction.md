# Reading change in evolving networks

Thank you for taking part.

You will be shown a visualization of a **network that changes over time** — a set of
people, the groups they belong to, and how both shift from week to week.

Your job is to answer questions about what changed and when. There are no trick
questions, and we are testing the **visualization, not you**. If something is
unclear or unreadable, that is a finding we want.

**What to expect**

- About **20 minutes**.
- A few background questions first, then a short guided tour.
- Then a series of short tasks. Each one shows a picture and asks one question.
- Please spend **about a minute per question**. If you are not sure, give your
  best answer and move on — a quick honest guess is more useful to us than a
  long deliberation.
- The picture changes between tasks. Please read each question carefully —
  they are not all the same.

**Before you start**

- Please use a **desktop or laptop** with the window as large as you can make it.
- Please work somewhere you will not be interrupted; we record how long each
  task takes.
- Please do not use notes, screenshots or another device.
