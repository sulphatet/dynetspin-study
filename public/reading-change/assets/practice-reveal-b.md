# Practice — the answer

**The answer was the large group** — eight of the outlined group's fifteen
characters are in it by Book 4.

One thing this small practice network could not show you: it has only three
groups in Book 4, of 10, 1 and 1 people, so the right answer was also the
biggest one. **In the real questions that will usually not be true** — the group
you are looking for is often middling in size, and sometimes small.

So do not answer by picking the biggest group. Note **where the outlined group
sits** and roughly how big it is before you move, then look for it again in the
later period.

Also worth knowing: not everyone survives. Only eight of the fifteen were still
in the network at all by Book 4. The question asks which group holds most of the
**same people**, not which group is closest in size.
