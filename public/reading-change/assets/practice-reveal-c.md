# Practice — the answer

**The largest group in Book 6 was the big one on the left**, holding 13 of the
18 characters.

That question only asked you to move to a period and click a group. The real
questions in this section ask something harder: you are shown one group in an
early period and asked **what happened to it** by a later one.

There are four answers, and they are defined on screen with every question, so
there is nothing to memorise. One distinction catches people out:

> A group whose members all stay in the network but **scatter into other
> groups** has **split**. **Dissolved** means the people themselves are gone
> from the data.

That was a practice question. The real ones start next.
