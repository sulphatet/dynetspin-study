# Practice — the answer

**The answer was the large group** — 13 of the 18 characters in Book 6 are in it.

That was just to check you can move to a period and click a group. The real
questions in this section ask something different: you are shown one group in an
early period and asked **what happened to it** by a later one.

The four answers are defined on screen with every question, so you do not need
to memorise them. One distinction is worth knowing in advance, because it is the
one that catches people out:

> A group whose members all stay in the network but **scatter into other
> groups** has **split**, not dissolved. **Dissolved** means the people
> themselves are gone from the data.
