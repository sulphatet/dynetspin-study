# 2 of 3 — Time

The network is shown **one time period at a time**.

The buttons down the **left-hand side** are the periods, oldest at the top.
Click one to jump to it. The one you are viewing is highlighted.

Move back and forth as much as you like — there is no limit, and no penalty for
checking a period twice.

In some tasks an extra control is available:

- a **slider**, which changes how the groups are arranged;
- a **round overview**, which shows every period at once.

If you do not see one of these in a particular task, it is not available for
that task. That is intentional, not a fault.
