Ego Network Across Time iEgo Spiral: each ring is a time step, each spoke is one neighbour, and each coloured wedge is one community.
- Dot: connected in that slice. No dot means not connected, so a gap in a spoke is someone who left and came back.
- ✕: connected up to the previous ring and never again.
- Colour of a dot: that neighbour's own presence pattern (stayed / left / joined / gapped).
- Wedge: the neighbour's community in the slice currently on screen — the same grouping the main chart draws as separate spirals, so the two views always agree. The bold ring marks that slice. Move to another slice and the wedges regroup.
- The ego's own community leads, starting at the top; the biggest communities get their own colour and the tail is pooled into "other".
- Order: longest-lasting neighbours first inside each wedge.
- Hover a spoke to follow one neighbour and see its community in every slice. Click a wedge to track this node plus that community in the Cohort Tracker, or the centre for the whole neighbourhood — the tracked node is drawn at the centre of the card with a heavy border, so two cards compare two ego networks.
