# One last part — and this one is open-ended

The tasks are over. There are no right answers from here on.

Next you will see **real data**: how countries voted together at the United
Nations between 1946 and 2015. Each dot is a country, and the groups are
countries that tended to vote the same way.

**Every feature of the tool is switched on** for this part, including ones you
may not have seen yet. Use whatever you like, or nothing at all.

**What we are asking.** Spend about **five minutes** looking around, then write
down whatever you noticed. For example — a country that changes group, a group
that appears or disappears, a period where a lot happens at once, or anything
that simply looks odd.

**We are not testing your history knowledge.** You do not need to know anything
about the UN. Describe what you see in the picture; if you happen to know what
it corresponds to, say that too, but it is not needed.

Short, blunt notes are more useful to us than polished sentences.
