# Reading change in evolving networks

Thank you for taking part.

You will be shown a visualization of a **network that changes over time** — a
set of people, the groups they belong to, and how both shift from period to
period. Your job is to answer questions about what changed and when. There are
no trick questions, and we are testing the **visualization, not you**. If
something is unclear or unreadable, that is a finding we want.

**What to expect**

- **About 25 minutes.** An optional extra section afterwards adds roughly
  fifteen more; you will be asked, and stopping before it costs you nothing.
- Consent, a few background questions, then one short page about the picture.
- Then three sets of tasks. Each set opens with one **practice question** that
  is **not scored** — you are shown the answer and why, either way.
- Each scored task shows a picture and asks one question. Give your best answer
  and move on — a quick honest guess is more useful than a long deliberation.
- The picture changes between tasks, so please read each question carefully.

**Before you start**

- Please use a **desktop or laptop**, with the window as large as you can make
  it.
- Please work somewhere you will not be interrupted; we record how long each
  task takes.
- Please do not use notes, screenshots or another device.

Click **Next** to continue.
